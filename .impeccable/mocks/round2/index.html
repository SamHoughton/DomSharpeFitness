Dark redesign options, round 2

Three different worlds. All keep your dark ground and chevron logo. None uses Oswald, amber-highlighted headlines, mono caption labels or hairline stat boxes. Click one to open it full size and scroll the whole mockup.

1. The Fight Bill

The scale versus the scan, set like a fight-night poster in heavy wood-type slab. The headline is the ruled bill band, the booking button is a ticket stub, and the other results stack beneath in shrinking type.

Risk: loud, and could read as boxing or gym-bro to a nervous beginner.

2. The Honours Board

A sign-written club honours board: dark green, gold lettering, Dom's portrait on a plaque, and each result entered like a record. The warmest and most British of the three.

Risk: could feel formal or traditional, less energetic.

3. The Work Print

Your real InBody printouts and photos as frames on a film strip, the scale's number struck through and the scan's circled in grease pencil. The most photo-led and editorial.

Risk: the film-editing idea is the least obvious to a first-time visitor.
