## Direction contract

THESIS: The whole site is one fight-night bill: the scale against the scan, set in heavy wood-type slab on black bill stock, ruled and stacked like a poster. It refuses the category dark-gym template (condensed caps with one highlighted word, small mono labels, hairline stat boxes) and the light programme card that was rejected.

OWN-WORLD: Black bill stock (#0e0c0a) with fine grain, cream ink (#eee1c3), and one arena gold (the brand #C8923A) kept for the winner, the VS mark, the ticket-stub booking button and the live selection. Bevan slab caps at collision scale for names and headings, Chivo for letterpress smalls and body. Heavy 4px double rules and 2px cream rules divide the sheet. Buttons are notched ticket stubs. Results are ranked by type size alone. The logo is untouched.

STORY: A nervous beginner or a racer sees the scale lose to the scan, believes the scan, finds Dom's real results ranked on the card, picks the door that fits them, and tears off the ticket to book the free consultation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 1440 by 900: header with the logo, nav and a gold ticket stub. Under it THE SCALE (dim cream, left) and THE SCAN (gold, right) in slab caps at about 156px in two lines, a gold VS roundel between them. Beneath, left: "-1.2 kg" struck with a gold bar; right: "-4.2% fat, +1.5 kg muscle". A ruled band carries the h1 "The scale isn't what matters." Then the lede, the undercard lines (body fat, muscle, weight in shrinking slab) and the gold ticket "Book your free consultation", with two route lines for beginners and racers below.
