Gyms in Letchworth: a factual guide

Reviewed: 11 August 2026. Prices and hours change, check current details before joining anywhere.

Letchworth Garden City has four main gyms beyond Dom's own studio. This page sets out what each one actually offers: facilities, price and hours, so you can compare before you commit.

Straight up front: this guide is written by Sharpe Strength. Dom coaches 1:1 from Studio 180 in Letchworth, so he has a stake in this. The facts below are the same regardless of who is reading them.

The quick answer

Nuffield Health, Letchworth Garden City

On Letchworth Lane, next to the Letchworth Hall Hotel, SG6 3NP. A full health club: cardio and resistance floors, group exercise classes, personal training, and a wet facilities side with a swimming pool, spa, sauna and steam room. There's also a swim school and a physiotherapy clinic on site, with free parking.

Open weekdays 06:00–22:00 and weekends 08:00–20:00 at the time of writing. Nuffield doesn't publish a flat headline membership price the way the budget gyms do, so check current rates directly.

PureGym Letchworth

Unit 3, Baldock Road, SG6 2HN. Open 24/7, advertised from £29.99 a month at the time of writing, no contract, with over 200 pieces of equipment covering free weights, fixed resistance machines and cardio. Free on-site parking.

Anytime Fitness also runs a 24/7 gym in Letchworth (SG6 3DU), so PureGym isn't the only round-the-clock option in town.

North Herts Leisure Centre

Baldock Road, SG6 2ER. Run by Everyone Active on behalf of North Herts Council. A recently refurbished gym with Life Fitness equipment, alongside a leisure pool with a wave machine and flume, a sports hall, squash courts and a full group exercise timetable. It's the option that covers a household with different interests under one membership.

The Letchworth Club

Spirella Building, Bridge Road, SG6 4ET. An independent, family-run gym built around free weights rather than cardio: competition-spec powerlifting kit (bench, calibrated plates, platforms) and strongman equipment (yoke, farmer's handles, axle bar, atlas stones, sandbags, dinnie handles). Both contract and no-contract memberships are available, with day passes from around £8 at the time of writing.

Questions

What is the cheapest gym in Letchworth?

PureGym advertises the lowest headline price, from £29.99 a month at the time of writing, with no contract. Always check current prices before joining, they change.

Is there a 24-hour gym in Letchworth?

Yes. PureGym Letchworth on Baldock Road is open 24/7. Anytime Fitness also operates a 24/7 gym in Letchworth.

Which Letchworth gym has a swimming pool?

Both North Herts Leisure Centre and Nuffield Health have swimming pools. North Herts Leisure Centre's is a public leisure pool with a wave machine and flume; Nuffield Health's is part of its spa facilities.

Is there a gym in Letchworth for serious strength training?

The Letchworth Club, at the Spirella Building, is an independent gym built around free weights, powerlifting and strongman equipment rather than cardio machines.

Do Letchworth gyms offer personal training?

Most of them do. Dom coaches 1:1 from Studio 180, a private training studio in Letchworth, and also from Bannatyne Fairfield in Hitchin. The first consultation is free.
