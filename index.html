What an hour with Dom actually looks like

Nobody tells you what happens when you walk in. Here it is, minute by minute.

  1. 0–10 min

    Talk

    Goals, injuries, what you’ve tried before and what hasn’t worked.

  2. 10–20 min

    Movement screen

    Dom watches you squat, hinge and press before loading anything up.

  3. 20–25 min

    InBody scan

    Your actual starting numbers, not a guess from the bathroom scale.

  4. 25–55 min

    Train

    Real lifting, coached rep by rep, at a level built around what you just showed him.

  5. 55–60 min

    What’s next

    What you do on your own before the next session, and why.

Got a question?

Do I need to already be fit to start?

Not at all. Dom works with all levels, from complete beginners to experienced lifters. The programme is built around where you are right now, not where you think you should be.

Where are sessions held?

Two venues: a full health club in Hitchin, and a private studio in Letchworth. Compare them here, then let Dom know which is easier for you.

How long is each session?

Sessions are typically 60 minutes. The first session is slightly longer as Dom will go through your goals, any injuries or limitations, and how you train currently.

What if I have an injury or health issue?

Let Dom know before your first session. He programmes around injuries and works closely with clients who have mobility issues, back pain, or long-term conditions. If something is outside his scope he will tell you honestly.

Will I get a nutrition plan?

Dom provides practical nutrition guidance as part of his coaching. Not a rigid meal plan, but realistic advice you can actually follow. He won’t tell you to cut out everything you enjoy.

How quickly will I see results?

Most clients notice a difference in how they feel within a few weeks: more energy, better movement, stronger in the gym. Visible physical changes typically follow after 6 to 8 weeks of consistent training and nutrition. Everyone’s different.

Can I cancel or reschedule sessions?

Yes. Dom asks for 24 hours notice where possible. Life happens, just message him directly and he will sort it out.
