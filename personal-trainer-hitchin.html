Questions

Do I need to already be fit to start?

Not at all. Dom works with all levels, from complete beginners to experienced lifters. The programme is built around where you are right now, not where you think you should be.

Do I need to be a Bannatyne member?

Ask Dom about access when you book. He will walk you through how sessions work at the club before your first visit, so there are no surprises on the day.

How quickly will I see results?

Most clients notice a difference in how they feel within a few weeks. Visible physical changes typically follow after 6 to 8 weeks of consistent training and nutrition.
