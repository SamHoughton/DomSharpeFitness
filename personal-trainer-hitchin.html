Personal Trainer in Hitchin

Dom Sharpe coaches 1:1 at Bannatyne Fairfield on Kingsley Avenue, Fairfield Park, Hitchin SG5 4JJ. The sessions happen in Hitchin itself. That sounds obvious, but some of the trainers who advertise for this town are actually based somewhere else entirely.

Training in Hitchin

Sessions run from Bannatyne Fairfield, a full health club with a proper weights floor and cardio kit, up at Fairfield Park on the north side of town. Dom coaches people from Fairfield Park, the town centre, Stotfold, Arlesey and Baldock, plus commuters who train before or after the trip to London.

Every session is 60 minutes, one to one. You get a programme written for you, technique coaching on every lift, and progress tracked with InBody body composition scans rather than guesswork and bathroom scales.
