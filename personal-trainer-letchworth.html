Personal Trainer in Letchworth

Dom Sharpe coaches 1:1 from Studio 180, a private personal training studio at Unit 8 Business Centre East, Fifth Avenue, Letchworth Garden City SG6 2TS. If the thought of a busy gym floor is the thing that has kept you from starting, this is the version of training without it.

A studio, not a gym floor

Training in a private studio changes the first six weeks more than people expect. There is no queuing for a rack, no music you did not choose at full volume, and nobody watching you learn a movement for the first time. It is just you, Dom and the session.

That makes it a good fit for beginners, for anyone coming back after years away, and for people who simply want their hour to be quiet and focused. Clients come from Letchworth, Baldock, Willian, Norton and the Stevenage side.

Questions

What is Studio 180 like?

A private personal training studio on Fifth Avenue in Letchworth Garden City. No crowds, no queuing for kit and nobody watching you train. It suits people who find big gym floors off-putting.

I am a complete beginner. Is that a problem?

No. Dom works with all levels and a private studio is honestly the easiest place to start. The programme is built around where you are right now.

Do I need my own equipment or a gym membership?

No membership is needed for the studio. It is set up specifically for personal training, so the kit you need for your session is there.

How do I book?

Send the consultation form, or message Dom on WhatsApp on 07375 219353. The first consultation is free and there is nothing to sign.
